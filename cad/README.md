# CAD notes

There are several revisions required for future printing, a lot of which are simply just for the shell of koganeee for design purposes and lore-accuracy. 
Some ideas I thought of while assembling the physical design:
1. The curvature of the head is fine but I noticed it can get too close to propellors sometimes, especially as the shell is too thin (scaling down the design would be vastly helpful, and it's definitely possible with smaller connectors on the FC)
2. My original intention was to hot glue or super glue the shell together which is fine but having more contact surface would've helped a lot more (and with better mechanical design, slots would be great as it would also allow reaching inward to debug)
3. I was originally going to use two servos (one of them powered) and both of them using a thread to carry the lower body, but I realized it's not very neat, so a meaningful addition would just be have a simple rail or pulley mechanism (lots of potential for mechanical improvements in this regard, I just didn't have the time to focus on this)
4. The shell is decently heavy, and for the size it makes sense but any material optimization would be great
5. Design feature but the wings on the head weren't designed to fit in well, just a physical guide on the head would work great
6. Otherwise, more time on Solidworks or Onshape can easily improve design quality (appearance) and mechanism feasibility!

Will add more notes here if I find any ideas and of course, open to suggestions!
